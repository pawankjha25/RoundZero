## Feedback on your draft post

Overall this is strong outreach copy — clear ask, concrete expectations for what's involved, transparent about it being free, and a clean CTA. A few small things I'd fix or consider before you post it:

- The trailing `?` at the very end is almost certainly a broken emoji (probably didn't survive copy/paste) — drop it or replace with a real one.
- Worth being precise about scope: this pilot's real depth is **Senior/Staff/Principal-level ML System Design** specifically, not "ML/AI interviews" broadly (Entry/Mid-level content and other round types aren't calibrated yet). Being upfront about that up front will get you better-fit applicants and avoid a mismatched tester finding the bar too high for what they expected.
- You mentioned wanting to record a 3–4 min video — worth a one-line reference to it in the post itself, since a short clip showing the product in action will convert much better than text alone for something this new.

## Video script — 3–4 min walkthrough

Bullet points to talk from, not a word-for-word script — a natural, slightly imperfect delivery will land better than a read-aloud script for something this early-stage.

**0:00–0:20 — Hook**
Prepping for a Staff/Principal ML System Design interview is hard to practice for — friends aren't calibrated to that bar, and generic mock-interview services don't go deep on ML system design specifically. That's the gap Round Zero is trying to close.

**0:20–1:00 — What it is**
Round Zero is an AI-powered mock interviewer. Pick your target role, level, and domain, and it runs a real, adaptive interview — it asks follow-ups based on what you actually say, the same way a real interviewer would probe a weak answer.

**1:00–2:30 — Live demo** (screen-record this part)
- Show the Setup screen: pick role family, level, domain, duration.
- Show a snippet of the interview itself — a question, your answer, and an adaptive follow-up.
- Cut to a finished report: dimension-by-dimension scores (1–4), the evidence narrative behind each score, the overall readiness % and hire signal.
- Show the Progress page briefly — the readiness-over-time chart and per-dimension trend tiles, since that's what "getting better over time" looks like.

**2:30–3:20 — What makes it different**
- Every score is backed by evidence from your actual transcript, not a generic rubric guess.
- It's calibrated against a real Senior/Staff/Principal bar, not graded on a curve.
- It's a loop, not a one-off — you can track whether you're actually improving across attempts.

**3:20–4:00 — The ask**
I'm looking for 4–5 early testers to try this out, free, in exchange for honest feedback and a short call afterward. Link's in the post/description — I'll reach out directly to whoever I select.
